# Is it a machined part?

Millr reviews machined parts: geometry made by taking material away from stock. Look at the geometry and the drawing the way a machinist would, not at the file name or what anyone called it, and decide which of three things the part is. Say which in the first stage line and in the receipt.

- **A machined part.** Review it.
- **A part made partly by another process, with machined features.** Review the machined features. The receipt's first line keeps SKILL.md's shape and names the other process: "Reviewed <file> as a part made partly by <process>: <what it is>, <material>, <size>. Only the machined features were reviewed."
- **Not a machined part.** Build no page. Post one message and stop: "Millr reviews machined parts. This is <what it is>, made by <process> (<what on the part or the drawing shows it>), so Millr does not review it. If it is meant to be machined, say so and it will be reviewed as one."
